## The Backrooms and the Backrooms Wiki

The Backrooms began as an anonymous post on 4chan's /x/ board in May 2019.

Content relating to the Backrooms in this game is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0.
The following concepts originate from the [Backrooms Wiki](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com) and its authors,
and have been adapted and changed for this game:

- **Level 0**: [Level 0 - "Threshold"](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-0) by DivineAtlas, DrAkimoto and RobertGoerman.
- **Smilers**: [Entity 3 - "Smilers"](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-3), originally by Palico22, rewritten by Stretchsterz.
- **Skin-Stealers**: [Entity 10 - "Skin-Stealers"](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-10) by AndromedaMapping1, rewritten by 1000dumplings.
- **Almond Water**: [Object 1 - "Almond Water"](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/object-1), originally by 1000dumplings, rewritten by Natedagreat563 and Poliacci.

The game follows the Backrooms Wiki [Licensing Guide](https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide):
it is released under GPLv3 with its full source public, keeps this credit to the wiki, and releases the art and
design documents based on these concepts under CC BY-SA 3.0.

**Original to this game:** the Howler and its design, the gameplay rules of all three entities (Smilers banished by
a flashlight beam, Skin-Stealers disguised as Almond Water), the tape logs, the landmarks and the arrow graffiti.

## How it was made

Built with Google AI Studio and Claude (Anthropic) under Jacky Yao's direction.

- The 3D models in `public/assets/models/` are built by Blender 4.5 scripts in `tools/blender/`, written with
  Claude Code under Jacky Yao's direction. They are original to this game.
- The concept images in `docs/design/art/concepts/` were made with an AI image tool at Jacky Yao's direction. Which
  tool, and when, is still to be recorded there.
